About Us

A Revolutionary Spirit

In response to the cultural upheaval that inspired the Arab Spring, Mate Way was founded in the spirit of that revolution to create a new standard for world class advertising. Not only did MWG manage to make its mark in Egypt, but it gained traction and popularity, forming firm partnerships and executing projects beyond Egypt's borders on the world stage. Building upon its outstanding creative momentum, MWG succeeded in extending its field of expertise, and influence, opening branches in major metropolitan cities including Paris, Dubai, and Hollywood.
